# Contributing to NormGuard

NormGuard is currently in **Phase 1: research and validation**. Contributions should strengthen the evidence behind the project before expanding the implementation.

## Working agreement

1. Open or claim an issue before beginning material work.
2. Create a focused branch such as `research/competitor-matrix`.
3. Keep one research question or deliverable per pull request.
4. Link every factual market or technical claim to a primary source where possible.
5. Separate observed evidence from team interpretation.
6. Include limitations, contradictions, and negative findings.
7. Request at least one review before merging.
8. Do not commit directly to `main`.

## Evidence standard

Research submissions must record:

- source title and canonical URL;
- publication or access date;
- exact capability supported by the source;
- limitation or uncertainty;
- relevance to NormGuard's proposed gap.

A missing result is useful evidence. Do not force a positive conclusion.

## Visual standard

Tables are preferred for exact comparisons. Diagrams are used for architecture, sequence, or decision flow. Charts must include units, sample size, labelled axes, accessible colours, and a caption stating what can—and cannot—be concluded.

Source data and chart-generation code must accompany final experimental figures.

## Pull-request checklist

- [ ] Scope matches a Phase 1 issue
- [ ] Claims have traceable sources
- [ ] Evidence and interpretation are clearly separated
- [ ] Diagrams and tables remain readable in GitHub light and dark modes
- [ ] Limitations and open questions are documented
- [ ] No private, licensed, or sensitive dataset has been committed
